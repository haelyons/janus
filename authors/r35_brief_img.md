Each page shows four units (rows a-d) of an image-classification network.

For each unit, from left to right: a synthetic image generated to make the unit as active as possible, then the five
photos (from a fixed set of about 5,000) that make it most active, strongest first. The square marks where in the
photo the unit is most active; the number under each photo is its activation, 0-10 relative to the strongest photo
shown in that row.

Some units may have no clear pattern.

Reply, per page, in exactly this form:

a: <what unit a responds to, one short phrase, or "can't tell">
b: <same for b>
c: <same for c>
d: <same for d>
shared: <what the four units have in common, one short phrase, or "nothing in common">
confidence: <1-5, how sure you are of these labels overall>

Label what the unit responds to as specifically as the photos allow (which part of an object, view, orientation,
shape, texture), not just the subject of the photos.
