OLMo Neuron Trajectories

24 MLP neurons of OLMo-2 7B, drawn at random (round 36, not picked by anyone). Each row is one training checkpoint: the three Pile documents where the neuron fired hardest, cut to 12 tokens each side of the peak. Overlap with final is the share of that checkpoint's top 16 documents that are also in the final base model's top 16.
