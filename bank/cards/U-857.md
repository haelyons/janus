Grid 3: pairs of photos with the same label in the dataset. For each of the 12 labels on which the unit is most active on average, the label's photo with the highest activation (cropped, red shading) beside its photo with the lowest activation (the whole photo).
Grid 4: photos on which the unit is strongly active (at least 30% of its maximum) although their label is not among the 200 labels (of 1000) on which it is most active on average; each with its label's average.
