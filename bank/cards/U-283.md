# Unit U-283

Recorded from one unit (a single neuron) in an image classification network, run over several thousand natural photos.
First attached grid: the 20 photos on which the unit is most active, strongest first, each cropped around the spot where it is most active, with red shading showing where it responds and raw activation values. Second attached grid: the 20 photos containing the spots that push the unit most strongly negative (its value before the ReLU, averaged over a small area), most negative first, each cropped around that spot, with blue shading showing where it is pushed negative.
Across all photos the unit is above 30% of its maximum on 5.4% of them.
Third attached grid: feature visualisations, synthetic images optimised to make one unit as active as possible. First the unit itself; then its eight strongest inputs from the layer below, by weight (the unit's value before the ReLU is exactly a weighted sum of 528 such inputs plus a constant; six positive, two negative); then the three units in the next layer that read from it with the largest positive weights.
