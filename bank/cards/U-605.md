# Unit U-605

Recorded from one unit (a single neuron) in an image classification network, run over several thousand natural photos. Activations are raw values; the strongest on these photos is 1527.
Grid 1: the 20 photos on which the unit is most active, strongest first, each cropped around the spot where it is most active, with red shading where it responds.
Grid 2: the 20 photos containing the spots that push the unit most strongly negative (its value before the ReLU, averaged over a small area), most negative first, each cropped around that spot, with blue shading.
Grid 3: pairs of photos with the same label in the dataset. For each of the 12 labels on which the unit is most active on average, the label's photo with the highest activation (cropped, red shading) beside its photo with the lowest activation (the whole photo).
Grid 4: photos on which the unit is strongly active (at least 30% of its maximum) although their label is not among the 200 labels (of 1000) on which it is most active on average; each with its label's average.
Grid 5: the unit's value before the ReLU is exactly a weighted sum of the 528 units in the layer below it, plus a constant. Each row is one of those input units: the six that contribute most when the unit fires (with each one's share of the positive contribution), then the two whose weighted input pushes the unit most negative on average. Each row shows that input unit's own six most active photos, cropped around where the input unit is most active, with its weight.
Across all photos the unit is above 30% of its maximum on 5.4% of them.
