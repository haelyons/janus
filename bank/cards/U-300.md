# Unit U-300

Recorded from one unit (a single neuron) in an image classification network, run over several thousand natural photos.
First attached grid: the 20 photos on which the unit is most active, strongest first, with raw activation values. Second attached grid: the 20 photos that push the unit most strongly negative (its value before the ReLU), most negative first, with raw values.
Across all photos the unit is above 30% of its maximum on 5.4% of them.
